<h1 align = "left">😄 Welcome to our Github!</h1>

We are the **${\rm BIO^2 DS}$ research group** (Biomedical Data Science & Biostatistics), a research group officially based at the [Faculty of Statistical Studies of the Complutense University of Madrid](https://estudiosestadisticos.ucm.es/). Our work focuses on the **analysis of data in the fields of medicine, health and its social determinants, omics and biology**, under the umbrella of data science, Machine Learning and biostatistics. We structure our research around three fundamental pillars: **methodological development**, **translational results in the aforementioned fields**, and **software production**.

<h2 align="left">🌱 What do we research? What are our current research lines?</h2>

In our group we research how to transform complex biomedical data into useful knowledge for clinical practice. We develop and apply robust and interpretable machine learning methods to analyze **omics data** (such as transcriptomics, genomics or immunome) and large-scale **clinical records**. Our current lines focus on the **development of predictive models** for complex biological phenomena —such as transplant rejection—, the analysis of immune repertoires in cancer, and risk estimation in clinical cohorts using **artificial intelligence** models adapted to **high-dimensional**, **compositional data** and highly heterogeneous contexts.
